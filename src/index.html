How I work

I do my best work when the scope is broad, the problems are messy and there isn't much process in the way. A lot of how I work now runs on AI. I prototype features in code myself, so engineers start from something real. I use AI for data analysis and forecasting, and I run lots of small experiments to test an idea before we commit to building it.

I've line managed PMs and designers and led a product and engineering function of 14. I put a lot of effort into making sure my teams are happy and proud of what they ship.

tiney

Product Manager August 2025 – Present

AI/Automation Specialist April 2025 – August 2025

NPS +5 → 50+

Childminder NPS after cutting first response times from 2+ days to under 4 hours

tiney is England's largest childminder agency. We recruit, train, register and support home-based childminders so more families can access great early years care, and the tiney app runs a childminder's whole business. I own product for growth, onboarding and operations, leading a squad of engineers alongside design. I joined on a contract to help the operations team work faster using AI and automation, then stayed on permanently as a PM.

  • Built tiney's Calendar, so childminders can manage schedules and invoices themselves. 63% of changes were self-serve in the first month, and invoice-related support messages fell 25%
  • Rebuilt the onboarding journey around the DBS check, the step that most often holds trainees up. The share submitting their DBS within 14 days of joining nearly doubled (18% to 33%)
  • Redesigned the lead gen form and call booking flow and A/B tested it with ~10k visitors: 16% more sales call bookings from the same traffic
  • Led the HMRC Making Tax Digital integration so childminders can submit their tax returns to HMRC from the app
  • Set up Intercom's Fin AI agent as our front line support chat, cutting first response times from 2+ days to under 4 hours. Childminder NPS went from +5 to over 50

Flux

Product Lead June 2021 – May 2022

0 → 65k users

Flux Offers, launched inside three months

Flux was a Series A stage fintech startup that provided itemised digital receipts and cashback rewards directly inside mobile banking apps like Monzo, Starling Bank and Barclays.

I led the product team at Flux, and was responsible for our Offers product and our Integrations team, who looked after Flux's APIs and our bank and retailer partnerships. We built and launched Flux Offers, which gives consumers instant cashback when they shop with their Flux-linked card, inside of three months. We then grew our user base from 0 to 65k users, opened up a stable revenue stream and onboarded record numbers of retailers to the Flux network as a result.
