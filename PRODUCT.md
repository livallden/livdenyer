# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Founders and CEOs of early-stage, mission-led startups who are hiring a product leader (Head of Product / CPO level). They usually arrive from a LinkedIn profile, an intro or a CV link, and spend a minute or two deciding whether Liv is worth a conversation.

## Product Purpose
liv.pm is Liv Allden's personal site: a single-page CV that shows a hiring founder what Liv has built, at what scale and with what results. Success is the founder connecting with Liv on LinkedIn.

## Positioning
Ten years in product, mostly fintech and payments, now at tiney (a childminder agency). Liv's pitch is 0→1 products at mission-led startups with measurable outcomes (iwocaPay idea to MVP in 3 months, Flux Offers 0 to 65k users, Tabeo Plans to 100+ practices and 14,000+ patients, 50% of Peblo underwriting automated), plus a hands-on, AI-assisted way of working: prototyping in code, AI data analysis, lots of small experiments.

## Operating Context
Visitors read on desktop between meetings or on a phone from a LinkedIn link. They scan for titles, companies, dates and numbers before they read any prose.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Eleventy static site with Tailwind CSS, deployed on Netlify. Content lives in `src/index.html`.
- Primary contact route is LinkedIn (linkedin.com/in/odenyer). No email address on the page.
- Liv is quietly open to new roles: a subtle signal, never the headline.
- There's a `/blog` route with no posts.

## Brand Commitments
- Name shown as Liv Allden.
- CV content stays as written: job titles, dates, companies and descriptions match Liv's current CV.
- The site is strongly themed around Liv's personality and says more about who she is, not just the CV.

## About Liv (confirmed by Liv, for the page)
- A big reader: about a book a week.
- A maker: usually knitting, crocheting or making pottery when not building software.
- Lives in the countryside with her husband and two cats; a big cat person. George is a skinny black-and-white rescue cat; Ellie is a white ragdoll with blue eyes.
- Takes trips in Betty, her green-and-white LDV Convoy camper van.

## Evidence on Hand
- CV content in `src/index.html` (roles, dates, results).
- Portrait photo: `src/static/img/liv.png`.
- Company logos: `src/static/img/` (iwoca, Flux, Peblo, KPMG, KCL, Triangirls). No tiney or Tabeo logos.
- No testimonials, references or press. Don't invent any.

## Product Principles
- Results first: numbers and outcomes are easier to scan than adjectives.
- Sound like a person, not a template. Liv's own voice is plain and direct.
- Be honest about status: currently at tiney, open to conversations.
- One clear next step: connect on LinkedIn.
