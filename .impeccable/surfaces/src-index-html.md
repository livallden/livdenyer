# Surface brief: liv.pm homepage

Scope: `src/index.html` (home), with the shared navbar and footer restyled for the home page. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: founders hiring a product leader. Action: connect on LinkedIn. Proof: CV roles and results, unchanged in substance. Plus who Liv is: reader, knitter/crocheter, potter, countryside, two cats, Betty the van.
Pinned by Liv (replaces the blanket direction, which she rejected as overloaded): a journey through the countryside; motifs of books, pottery, cats, van trips, knitting, crochet; clean, simple, calming; serif fonts. Journey runs now → back in time. Photo near the top.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Reading Liv's CV is a slow drive down a country lane: the opening scene is a quiet landscape, each role is a stop along the road, and the lane ends at home with the things she loves. It refuses the card grid and the busy, decorated CV.

OWN-WORLD: Pale morning-sky ground easing into meadow green, never cream. Deep hedgerow-green ink, Betty green and white, one wheat-ochre accent. Motif-only fills may also use poppy #c4553c, clay #c98a64 and sky blues, never on UI. Achievement fingerposts: white boards beside each stop. Flat, softly layered hills; thin single-weight line drawings with flat fills for motifs. Young Serif headings, Literata body. Lots of air; no cards, borders or boxes around content.

STORY: Who Liv is and what she's done is clear in the first viewport, then her career unrolls as stops down the lane, then she becomes a person (books, pottery, yarn, cats, Betty), and the evening close asks the visitor to connect on LinkedIn.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Text on the sky at left: small round photo, "Liv Allden" large in Young Serif, a one-line pitch, a short paragraph with her headline results, a Betty-green LinkedIn button and a quiet "open to conversations" line. A full-width hill landscape fills the lower part, with a winding lane, Betty on it and a cat on a fence post.

FORM: seed:18fe20d6ab31a661, direction pinned by user over the roll (the rolled blanket direction was built and rejected). Signature interaction: Betty, seen from above, drives down the lane at the left of the journey as you scroll (sticky), reaching each stop in turn.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
